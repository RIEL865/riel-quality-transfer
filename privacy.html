RIEL Quality Transfer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

1. Videos and Files

The transfer feature reads selected videos locally in your browser for previewing and saving. When you choose the TikTok posting feature, the selected video is intentionally sent to TikTok through the TikTok Content Posting API so you can publish it to the TikTok account you authorize.

2. TikTok Authorization

If you connect TikTok, RIEL Quality Transfer uses TikTok Login Kit to obtain permission to post on your behalf. The TikTok access and refresh tokens are handled by the server and are not intentionally exposed to the page's JavaScript.

3. Personal Information

The transfer feature does not intentionally request your name, phone number, address, or payment information. TikTok may provide basic account information needed for the posting flow, such as your TikTok display name.

4. Third-Party Services

The website may be hosted by Vercel or GitHub Pages depending on deployment. TikTok processes content and account authorization when you use the TikTok integration. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Cookies

The TikTok integration uses a secure, HTTP-only session cookie to maintain the authorization session. The site does not use cookies for advertising.

6. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date above shows when it was last updated.

7. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the contact method provided with the service.
